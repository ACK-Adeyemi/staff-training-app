## Giving medicines safely

Every setting must have a **medicines policy and procedures**. Staff follow them every time.

- Keep up-to-date information about each child's medicine needs.
- Give a medicine only with **written permission** from the parent or carer for **that particular medicine**. This covers prescription and non-prescription medicines.
- Prescription medicines must have been **prescribed for that child** by a doctor, dentist, nurse or pharmacist. Medicines containing aspirin are only given if a doctor prescribed them.
- If giving a medicine needs medical or technical knowledge, staff must have **training**.

---

## Records and telling parents

- Write a **record each time** a medicine is given.
- Tell the parent or carer **the same day**, or as soon as reasonably practicable.
- There is also a procedure for ill or infectious children, discussed with parents and carers. It covers how to stop infection spreading.

---

Key points:
- No written permission, no medicine.
- Record every dose and tell the parent the same day.
- Only prescribed medicine, only for the child it was prescribed for.
