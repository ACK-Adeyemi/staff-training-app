## Weaning and seating

- Talk with parents and carers about the stage a child is at with **solid food** (weaning) and the textures they know. Never decide by age alone.
- Babies and young children should sit safely in a highchair or a suitably sized low chair. Where possible, use a set eating space with fewer distractions.

---

## Preventing choking

- Prepare food in a way that prevents choking.
- Children must always be within **sight and hearing** of a member of staff while eating.
- **Choking can be completely silent.** Where possible, sit facing the children.
- A member of staff with a valid paediatric first aid certificate should always be in the room while children eat.
- If a child needs help after choking, record where and how it happened and make sure parents and carers know.

---

Key points:
- Stay with the children and watch while they eat.
- Choking is not always noisy.
- Record any choking incident that needed help.
