## Allergies and dietary needs

Before a child starts, the setting must find out about their special dietary needs, preferences, food allergies and intolerances, and any special health needs. This is shared with **all staff who prepare and handle food**.

At each mealtime and snack time, it must be clear who is responsible for checking each child's food and drink.

---

## Allergy action plans

- The setting works with parents and carers, and with health professionals where appropriate, to make an **allergy action plan** for each known allergy.
- Plans are kept up to date and shared with all staff.
- Staff must know the symptoms and treatments for allergies and anaphylaxis, and the difference between an allergy and an intolerance.
- Children can develop allergies at any time, especially when they first have solid foods.

---

Key points:
- Know the children's allergies before the meal, not during it.
- Follow the allergy action plan.
- Ask if you are not sure who checks the food.
