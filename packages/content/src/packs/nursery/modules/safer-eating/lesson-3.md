## Food hygiene and food poisoning

Food areas must be equipped for hygienic preparation, with suitable sterilising equipment for babies' food. Everyone who prepares or handles food must be competent and have food hygiene training.

---

## Food poisoning

If two or more children cared for on the premises get **food poisoning**, the registered provider must tell Ofsted as soon as reasonably practicable, and in any event within **14 days**.

---

Key points:
- Food hygiene training is needed for everyone who prepares or handles food.
- Tell your manager at once if more than one child has a stomach upset after a meal.
