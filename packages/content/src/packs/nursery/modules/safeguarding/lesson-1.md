## Everyone has a part to play

Every person looking after children must be alert to any issues of concern in a child's life, at home or elsewhere. That includes you, whatever your job.

---

## The designated safeguarding lead

Every setting must have a practitioner designated to take lead responsibility for safeguarding children. This person is the **designated safeguarding lead**, or DSL.

The DSL:

- leads on safeguarding in your setting
- links with local children's services and the Local Safeguarding Partners
- gives you support, advice and guidance, on an ongoing basis and on any specific safeguarding issue

---

Key points:
- Everyone looking after children must be alert to concerns.
- The DSL takes the lead, but safeguarding is not only the DSL's job.
- Go to your DSL for advice, whenever you need it.

---

> Take your time. If you are not sure whether something is a concern, ask your DSL. Asking is always the right thing to do.

---
