## Phones and cameras

Every safeguarding policy must set out how mobile phones, cameras and other devices that can take and share images are used in the setting. Follow your setting's rules. If you are unsure whether you may use a device, ask first.

---

## Suitable people

People looking after children must be suitable. Staff and people who want to work with children must tell the provider about anything that may affect their suitability, such as arrests, charges, convictions, cautions, court orders, reprimands and warnings.

Nobody may start work or volunteering until their enhanced criminal records and barred list check has come back. Until a person has been checked, they must not have unsupervised contact with the children.

---

## Keeping your training up to date

All practitioners must be trained in safeguarding, and providers must support you to feel confident using the policy. The framework says safeguarding training must be renewed every two years. Some settings add a shorter refresher in between to keep skills fresh.

---

Key points:
- Follow your setting's rules on phones, cameras and other devices.
- Suitability checks come first: no unsupervised time with children until they are done.
- Safeguarding training is renewed every two years.
