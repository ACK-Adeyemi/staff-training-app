## The assembly point

Once outside, everyone goes straight to the **assembly point**. At Little Acorns this is the front garden, by the blue gate.

- Stay with your group. Children must not wander off.
- Do not go back inside for any reason. Only the fire brigade goes back in.
- Keep the road clear for the fire engines.

---

## The register and roll call

The person in charge needs to know everyone is out.

- Each Room Leader takes the **register** for their room and calls the names of the children, using the room list or the daily register.
- Practitioners, students, visitors and contractors are counted too. The **visitors' book** tells you who was in the building.
- Tell the person in charge straight away if anyone is missing. Say who they are and where they were last seen.
- Give the information to the fire brigade when they arrive. Never go back in to look.

---

## Staying calm and informing parents

- Keep the children together and calm. Sing a song or count aloud.
- Do not let parents take their child away until the roll call is finished and the person in charge has agreed.
- The person in charge decides when it is safe to go back in. Nobody goes in until the fire brigade says so.
- Afterwards the person in charge records the drill or incident in the fire log and tells parents what happened.

---

Key points:
- Go straight to the assembly point and stay there.
- Room Leaders do the roll call and tell the person in charge if anyone is missing.
- Nobody goes back in until the fire brigade says so.
