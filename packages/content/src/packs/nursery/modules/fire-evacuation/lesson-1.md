## Raising the alarm

If you see fire or smell smoke, act straight away. Do not wait to be sure.

- Sound the alarm by pressing the nearest **red call point**.
- Tell the person in charge of the setting. At Little Acorns this is the Nursery Manager, or the Deputy Manager if the manager is away.
- Do not stop to collect belongings, and do not try to put out a fire unless it is very small and you have been trained.

---

## Who does what

- The **person in charge** makes sure the fire brigade is called on 999.
- The **Room Leader** in each room takes charge of getting the children out.
- Everyone else helps the Room Leader and keeps calm. Children copy how adults behave.

---

Key points:
- Press the nearest call point as soon as you see fire or smell smoke.
- Tell the person in charge. They call 999.
- Never stop to collect belongings.
