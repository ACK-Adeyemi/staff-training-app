## Sleep: what the framework says now

The framework sets out how babies and children under 2 must be placed down to sleep safely:

- On their **back**, in their own separate sleep space.
- Babies aged 12 months and under only in a cot.
- Cots clear of toys, pillows, extra blankets, bumpers, wedges and straps.
- Children within sight and hearing of staff, and checked often.
- Babies under six months have an adult in the same room for every sleep.

## Screen use

Providers must have regard to the Government's guidance on children's **screen use** in early years settings. Ask your manager how your setting follows it.
