## Banned dogs

Banned dog breeds, meaning dogs to which section 1 of the **Dangerous Dogs Act** 1991 applies, including the **XL Bully** type, must not be kept or present on the premises at any time. This is still true if the dog's owner has a certificate of exemption.

---

## Volunteers and checks

Settings must get an enhanced criminal records check and barred list check for every person aged 16 and over, including all volunteers, who works directly with children, lives on the premises or works there. Nobody may begin working or volunteering until the check has come back.

---

## Regulated activity

The **Crime and Policing Act 2026** removed the old exemption for supervised unpaid activity. Paid and unpaid activities such as teaching, training, caring for or supervising children are now **regulated activity** if done frequently, on more than 3 days in a **30-day** period, or overnight. Helping a child with eating, drinking, toileting, washing or dressing is personal care.

---

Key points:
- No banned dogs on the premises, ever.
- Volunteers need their checks before they start.
- Regular unpaid helpers can count as regulated activity. Ask your manager.
