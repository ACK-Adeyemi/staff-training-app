## Paediatric first aid at your setting

Paediatric first aid, or PFA, is first aid for babies and young children.

At least one person with a current PFA certificate must be on the premises and available **at all times** when children are present. That person must also go with children on **outings**.

---

## Certificates

- The certificate must be for a full course that suits people caring for young children and babies.
- PFA training must be renewed **every three years**.
- If you gained a level 2 or level 3 qualification since 30 June 2016, you need a PFA qualification within three months of starting work to be counted in the staff:child ratios.
- Settings should display PFA certificates, or make them available to parents and carers, or share a list of staff with a current certificate.

---

Key points:
- Someone with a current PFA certificate is always on the premises when children are present.
- They also go on outings.
- Certificates are renewed every three years.
