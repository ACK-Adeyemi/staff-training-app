## Accidents and first aid records

A first aid box with suitable items for children must always be easy to reach.

When a child has an accident or injury, or needs first aid:

- the setting must keep a **written record** of the accident or injury and any first aid given
- parents and carers must be told on the **same day**, or as soon as reasonably practicable after, and told about any first aid given

---

## Serious accidents

If a child has a serious accident, illness or injury while in the setting's care, the registered provider must tell Ofsted what happened and what action was taken. This must be as soon as reasonably practicable and in any event within **14 days**. The provider must also tell local child protection agencies and follow their advice.

---

Key points:
- Write it down: accidents, injuries and first aid are always recorded.
- Tell parents and carers the same day, or as soon as reasonably practicable after.
- Tell your manager about any serious accident straight away. They deal with telling Ofsted.
