## Keeping everyone safe

On your first day, find out where the **fire exits** are, where the **assembly point** is and what to do when the alarm sounds. Look for the **first aid kit** and the names of the trained first aiders.

---

## Sign in, sign out

Make sure you know how children are signed in and out, who may collect them, and how visitors are recorded. If someone you do not know asks to collect a child, do not hand the child over. Ask your manager.

---

Key points:
- Know the fire exits and the assembly point.
- Know where the first aid kit is.
- Never hand a child to someone you do not know. Ask your manager.

---

> Your setting will have its own routines. Ask your manager to show you how they work here.
