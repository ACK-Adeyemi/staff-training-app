## Welcome

Your first days are about getting to know the children, the people and the way things are done in your setting. Nobody expects you to know it all at once.

- Learn the **names** of the children in your room and who their key person is.
- Find out who your **manager** is and who to ask when you are not sure.
- Ask to see the setting's **policies**. They tell you what to do and what not to do.

---

## Who to tell when you are worried

Every setting has a **designated safeguarding lead**. If you are worried about a child, tell them straight away. Do not wait, and do not try to deal with it alone.

---

Key points:
- Ask when you are not sure.
- Know who your manager and your safeguarding lead are.
- Tell the safeguarding lead at once if you are worried about a child.
