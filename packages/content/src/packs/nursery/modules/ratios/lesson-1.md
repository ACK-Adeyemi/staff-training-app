## Staff to child ratios

The ratios are the **minimum** number of staff for the number of children. They apply to the total number of staff available to work directly with children across the whole provision, not just each room.

- Children **under two**: at least one member of staff for every **three** children.
- Children aged **two**: at least one member of staff for every **five** children.
- Children aged three and over: the ratio depends on whether a qualified teacher or an Early Years Professional or Teacher is working with them, and on the type of setting. Ask your manager which applies here.

---

## Who counts

- Nobody aged **under 17** counts in the ratios, except apprentices from the age of 16. Nobody under 17 may look after children unsupervised.
- The manager must hold an approved qualification at level 3 or above, and at least half of all other staff must hold at least an approved level 2.
- Staff with an Early Years Educator qualification also need a suitable level 2 in English to count at level 3.
- Children must usually be within **sight and hearing** of staff, and always within sight or hearing.

---

Key points:
- One to three for under twos. One to five for twos.
- Under 17s do not count, apart from apprentices from 16.
- Ratios cover the whole setting.
