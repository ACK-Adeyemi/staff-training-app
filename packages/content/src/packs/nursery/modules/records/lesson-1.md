## Looking after children's information

Settings keep records so they are **safe and run well** and so each child's needs are met. They share accurate information with parents and carers, and with other professionals when appropriate.

- Keep records **easy to find** but **held securely**.
- Only people with a **right or professional need** may see confidential records about children and staff.
- All staff must understand the need to **protect children's privacy**.
- Settings must know their duties under **data protection law**.

---

## What is recorded, and who can see it

- For each child: full name, date of birth, parent or carer names and addresses, who has parental responsibility, who the child lives with, and emergency contacts (more than two numbers where possible).
- Parents and carers can see **all records about their child**, unless an exemption applies.
- Records about a child are kept for a **reasonable time** after they leave. Your manager knows how long.

---

Key points:
- Keep records secure and share them only with people who need them.
- Protect children's privacy, every time.
- Parents can see their own child's records.

---
