## A safe sleep space

For children under 2 years old, babies and children must be placed down to sleep safely.

- Place each child on their **back**, in their own separate sleep space, on a firm flat surface such as a cot, bed or mattress on the floor.
- Babies aged **12 months** and under must only be placed to sleep in a **cot**. Cots include cots, carrycots, Moses baskets and travel cots.
- Never cover a child's head.

---

## What goes in the sleep space

- Only a firm, flat, waterproof mattress and lightweight bedding, firmly tucked in below the child's shoulders. A well fitted baby sleep bag can be used instead. Check the maker's advice first.
- If you use a blanket, place the child **feet-to-foot** at the bottom of the cot, with the blanket tucked in.
- No extras: no toys, pillows, extra blankets, **bumpers**, wedges or straps.

---

Key points:
- On their back, in their own sleep space.
- Babies of 12 months and under sleep in a cot.
- Keep the cot clear.
