## Checking children while they sleep

- Children must always be within **sight and hearing** of staff when sleeping.
- All children must be **checked often** while they sleep.
- Babies under **six months** old must always have an adult with them in the same room for every sleep.

---

## Not too hot, not too cold

Children should not get too hot or too cold. The recommended **room temperature** for babies is 16 to 20 degrees C. Children's heads must not be covered.

---

Key points:
- Always within sight and hearing.
- Check often.
- Under six months: an adult in the room for every sleep.
