## Keeping children safe from risks

Providers must take all reasonable steps to make sure staff and children are **not exposed to risks**, and must be able to show how they are managing them.

- A risk assessment looks at what could cause harm, and the steps to **remove, minimise and manage** it.
- Providers decide where a written risk assessment helps staff practice and shows parents, carers or inspectors how risks are managed.
- It should say which parts of the environment need regular checks, when, and by whom.

---

## On outings

- Children must be kept safe on outings. Assess the risks and hazards **before** you go.
- The assessment must include **staff to child ratios**.
- An outing risk assessment does not have to be in writing. That is up to the provider.
- Keep in mind the paediatric first aid requirements while you are out.
- Any vehicle carrying children, and its driver, must be adequately insured.

---

Key points:
- Assess the risks before every outing, including ratios.
- Know how your setting records its risk assessments.
- Report anything unsafe to your manager.

---

> Your setting has its own risk assessments and outing forms. Ask your manager where to find them.
